Our Approach


We know the online retailing landscape because we’re part of it. We’ve honed our methodology on our own product line, so we know firsthand what works — and what doesn’t. While competing firms simply provide the connection to the marketplace, we back our technology with our significant expertise in logistics, buying behavior, inventory planning and merchandising. Our clients count on us to actively manage the online sales channel and leverage data to make smart business decisions.

We understand that online retail is not “one size fits all”. This is why we work with clients to determine what services are best for them. We partner with manufacturers and retailers to strengthen their digital go-to-market strategies, increase decision-making efficiency and positively impact profitability.
